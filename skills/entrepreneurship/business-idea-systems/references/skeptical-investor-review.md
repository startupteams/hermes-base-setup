# Skeptical Investor Review Process

This document outlines the specific skeptical investor review process applied to business ideas during this session, based on the "Skeptical Optimizer" AI Angel Investor framework for 2026-2027 seed/pre-seed investments.

## Core Philosophy
"Show me the proprietary data, not the LLM demo." - Assume every pitch is overhyped and technology is commoditized.

## Key Investment Pillars Applied
1. **Data Moats > Model Hype**: Prioritized startups with unique, proprietary data access
2. **Vertical Integration**: Favored Agentic AI/Full-Stack approaches that redesign workflows
3. **Unit Economics**: Strict focus on COGS, inference costs, and clear paths to profitability
4. **Human-in-the-Loop**: Preferred solutions that augment humans rather than fully autonomous systems

## The Four Mandatory Questions
Each idea was required to answer these specific technical questions:

1. **"If I give you $500k, how much of it goes to OpenAI APIs versus proprietary development?"**
   - Looked for <15-20% going to API costs in early versions
   - Preferred open-source model strategies with clear paths to reducing API dependency
   - Valued specific plans for proprietary data collection and model fine-tuning

2. **"Which specific, non-public dataset are you utilizing to train this model?"**
   - Required concrete, ethically-sourced proprietary data
   - Looked for data that creates network effects or continuous improvement
   - Examples: Job market data from hiring managers, support ticket corrections, expert-validated knowledge bases

3. **"When OpenAI releases a model that does exactly this, what is your unfair advantage?"**
   - Sought advantages beyond the model itself (data, workflow, network effects)
   - Valued proprietary datasets that competitors can't easily replicate
   - Looked for human-in-the-loop elements that add contextual understanding

4. **"Why does this need to be an AI company? Could this be solved with a good Python script?"**
   - Required clear explanation of why static rules or scripts aren't sufficient
   - Valued continuous learning, personalization, or complex reasoning needs
   - Looked for marketplace dynamics or data flywheels that exceed static capabilities

## Red Flags Screened For
- ❌ "We are the Uber for X + AI" - Generic LLM applications
- ❌ "We have no API costs, we built our own model" - Unrealistic, high burn rate
- ❌ Founder cannot explain *how* their AI works - Relies only on ChatGPT/Claude APIs
- ❌ No clear, immediate pain point - Solving a "nice to have" problem

## Implementation in Idea Documents
Each idea received these additional sections:

### Data Moat & Proprietary Advantage
- Specific description of proprietary data sources
- Explanation of how data creates self-improving systems
- Details on network effects or continuous learning mechanisms

### Human-in-the-Loop Approach
- Clear delineation of where humans augment AI
- Liability reduction strategies
- Personalization and contextualization mechanisms

### Unit Economics
- Detailed COGS breakdown (especially AI inference costs)
- Realistic pricing strategies and margin projections
- Specific optimization techniques (caching, model selection, etc.)

### FAQ for Skeptical Investors
- Direct answers to the four mandatory questions above
- Idea-specific data sources, cost allocations, and defensibility explanations

## Validation Workflow Integration
This review process fits into the broader idea validation workflow:

1. **Initial Customer Discovery** (Mom Test interviews)
2. **Skeptical Investor Review** (Add the four sections above)
3. **MVBP Definition** (Minimum Viable Business Product)
4. **Assumption Testing** (Fake-door tests, landing page experiments)
5. **Iteration or Pivot** (Based on evidence from steps 1-4)

The skeptical investor review doesn't replace customer discovery—it adds a critical layer of business model rigor that helps identify weaknesses early, before significant development investment.
