## Part 3: Open GPU Racks vs 4U Chassis

### The Problem with 4U Chassis
- **Enclosed airflow**: 4U chassis trap heat from open-air axial cards (5060 Ti)
- **Limited slot spacing**: Multi-slot GPUs block adjacent PCIe slots
- **Riser complexity**: To use blocked slots, you need PCIe risers
- **Rack fan dependency**: Blower cards survive, but passive/axial cards need chassis airflow

### Open GPU Rack Advantages
- **Zero chassis heat**: GPUs sit in open air with ambient room airflow
- **No slot blocking**: Each GPU has unlimited vertical clearance
- **No risers needed** (if using direct PCIe slots on mining frames)
- **Cheaper**: Mining frames cost $50-100 vs $200-500 for 4U chassis
- **Thermal performance**: ~10-15°C lower GPU temps vs enclosed 4U

### Open GPU Rack Disadvantages
- **PCIe risers reduce bandwidth**: x16 risers typically lose 2-5% bandwidth, x1 risers lose 80%+
  - **For EP strategy**: Minimal impact — experts are independent, minimal inter-GPU traffic
  - **For TP strategy**: Significant impact — TP requires allreduce communication
  - **Your case (EP)**: ✅ Riser bandwidth loss is negligible for EP MoE
- **No PSU integration**: Need external PSU mounting
- **No noise containment**: Open rack = full GPU noise in the room
- **Dust exposure**: Open cards accumulate dust faster
- **No hot-swap**: Changing GPUs requires shutting down

### Recommendation

For your EP-based MoE inference, **open GPU racks are viable** if:
1. You use **x16 PCIe riser cables** (not x1 risers) — these maintain ~95% bandwidth
2. You switch to **blower-style GPUs** (3080 Turbo) which don't need chassis airflow
3. The server room is isolated (noise isn't an issue)
4. You add **inline dust filters** on intake fans

**For your current setup**: If you sell the 5060 Tis and A2s and replace with 3080 Turbos, the 4U chassis will work well because 3080 Turbos are blower cards that exhaust heat directly out the back. The 4U chassis approach is simpler and more professional.

**If you later add RTX 3090s** (open-air axial), then open GPU racks become necessary because 3090s dump heat into the chassis and need maximum airflow.
