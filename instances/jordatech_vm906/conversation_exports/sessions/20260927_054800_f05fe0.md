## 2. Recommended production layout

Use immutable releases:

```text
/opt/llm-manager/
├── releases/
│   ├── <git-sha-1>/
│   └── <git-sha-2>/
├── current -> /opt/llm-manager/releases/<active-sha>
```

```text

├── secrets/
├── tls/
├── litellm.env
```

Benefits: exact Git-SHA identity, atomic-ish promotion, simple rollback, no untracked production edits.
