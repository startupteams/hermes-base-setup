# AgentifyMe.com AI-as-a-Service

**Unique identifier:** `agentifyme-ai-as-a-service`

## One-line summary

A managed AI-as-a-Service company that sets up, hosts, monitors, and improves personalized AI agents for two well-defined customer segments — Midwest US real-estate brokerages wanting to scale their agent workforce, and time-poor entrepreneurs running multiple revenue streams — for a predictable monthly fee.

## Beachhead Persona 1: Real-Estate Brokers in the Midwest US

**Who they are:** Independent or small-team real-estate brokerages in Midwest states (Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Michigan, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Missouri, Iowa) with 5-30 licensed agents. They want to grow to 50+ agents and 5-10M in transaction volume. They compete with national franchises (Keller Williams, RE/MAX, eXp) and local dominant brokerages but have stronger relationships and local knowledge.

**The pain:**
- Recruiting new agents is expensive and inconsistent. Open houses don't convert to hires. Cold outreach has low response.
- Onboarding new agents takes 60-90 days of broker time: compliance training, CRM setup, marketing guidance, deal process, MLS training, local market education.
- Experienced agents leave for franchises or teams — and take their knowledge, client lists, and processes with them.
- Brokerages lack automated recruitment funnels, standardized onboarding sequences, CRM follow-up discipline, and performance tracking across agents.
- The broker is the bottleneck: every agent question, every lead follow-up, every scheduling conflict requires broker attention.

**The managed-agent solution:**
1. **Recruiting Agent:** AI that captures, scores, and nurtures prospective agent leads from open houses, social media, and website forms. Qualifies on experience, availability, income goals, and commitment level before handing to a human recruiter.
2. **Onboarding Agent:** AI-powered new-agent playbook that walks hires through their first 90 days: compliance checklists, CRM setup, local market guides, deal templates, scheduling rules, weekly milestones, and automated check-ins with the broker.
3. **Agent Performance Agent:** Tracks each agent's leads, conversions, activity, and productivity. Sends weekly reports to the broker with flagged underperformers, recommended interventions, and coaching suggestions.
4. **Broker Delegation Agent:** Handles routine broker requests — schedule a meeting, find an agent's contact info, pull a transaction update, draft a message to a prospective hire — so the broker spends time growing the business, not administrating it.

## Beachhead Persona 2: Entrepreneurs Who "Burn the Midnight Oil"

**Who they are:** Bootstrapped entrepreneurs and solopreneurs running 1-5 revenue streams (side hustles, freelancing, digital products, e-commerce, consulting). They work 60-80 hour weeks across their ventures. They have some technical awareness (they know what ChatGPT, Zapier, and Make.com are) but lack the time, discipline, or expertise to maintain complex automations.

**The pain:**
- They built automations in ChatGPT Custom GPTs, Zapier, Make.com, or n8n but they break, stagnate, or become obsolete as their business changes.
- They don't have time to debug broken integrations, update prompts, clean data, or retrain AI agents.
- They want to build more businesses and multiply revenue but are bottlenecked by 24-hour days.
- They've heard about "AI agents" but don't know which ones to build, how to connect them, or how to monitor whether they're generating ROI.
- DIY tools require constant maintenance — they bought the tools but not the time.

**The managed-agent solution:**
1. **Content & Lead Engine Agent:** Maintains and monitors the entrepreneur's lead-generation and content systems — social media scheduling, blog post pipelines, email sequences, lead capture forms, follow-up sequences. The agent watches for broken links, stale content, low-engagement patterns, and flagged errors.
2. **Customer Service Agent:** Handles customer inquiries, FAQs, support tickets, order status, and booking requests across all the entrepreneur's ventures. Monitors for escalation triggers and routes exceptions to the owner.
3. **Finance & Ops Agent:** Tracks revenue by venture, monitors expenses, sends weekly profit/loss snapshots, flags cash-flow issues, and prepares monthly tax-summary reports. Monitors subscription renewals and recurring revenue.
4. **Growth Experiment Agent:** Runs low-risk growth experiments — tests new channels, tries new messaging, analyzes conversion rates, and recommends which experiments to scale or kill based on actual data.

## Product Concept

AgentifyMe.com provides managed AI agents as a service, tailored to two distinct verticals.

**Core workflow (both beachheads):**

1. Diagnose the buyer's top repetitive workflow through a short process audit.
2. Scope one narrow agent: recruiting agent, onboarding agent, performance agent, content engine, customer service, or ops agent.
3. Build using existing AI and automation infrastructure: OpenAI/Claude, n8n/Make/Zapier, CRM/helpdesk/calendar/email integrations, vector search, and logging.
4. Host and monitor the agent under a managed service agreement.
5. Provide monthly optimization: usage report, failures, human handoffs, updated knowledge, cost report, and ROI summary.
6. Expand only after one agent proves value.

**Positioning for Beachhead 1:**
> "Hire more agents, onboard them faster, and grow your brokerage without burning out your broker."

**Positioning for Beachhead 2:**
